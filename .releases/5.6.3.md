**Bug Fixes**

* Corrected issue with `DynamoProvider.scan` that caused incomplete results to be erroneously returned in some cases (e.g. a subsequent page of results cannot be deserialized or fails with a non-retryable error). Now, in these cases, a rejected promise will be returned.
* Corrected issue with `DynamoProvider.query` that caused incomplete results to be erroneously returned in some cases (e.g. a subsequent page of results cannot be deserialized or fails with a non-retryable error). Now, in these cases, a rejected promise will be returned.
* Corrected regression to the handling of `input` to a `promise.pipeline` invocation (promises are now unwrapped again).

**Other**

* Removed hard-coded region fallback from `LambdaSecretsManager` and instead allow `AwsOptions` to manage the fallback.
* Removed hard-coded region fallback from `LambdaResponseGeneratorS3` and instead allow `AwsOptions` to manage the fallback.
